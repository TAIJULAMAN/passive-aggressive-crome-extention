# Privacy Policy for Passive-Aggressive Duck

**Last Updated:** January 26, 2026

## Overview
Passive-Aggressive Duck is a Chrome extension that provides passive-aggressive coding motivation. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection
**We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The extension operates entirely offline and does not communicate with any external servers.

## Local Storage
The extension uses Chrome's storage API for:
- **User Preferences**: Mute status, custom insults, sensitivity settings
- **Visit Tracking**: Website visit counts (stored locally only, never transmitted)
- **Settings Sync**: Optional Chrome sync for settings across your devices

All data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your computer unless you enable Chrome's built-in sync feature.

## Permissions Explained

### `storage`
Used to save your preferences locally, including:
- Mute/unmute status
- Custom passive-aggressive messages you create
- Visit counts for StackOverflow tracking

### `<all_urls>`
Required to inject the duck on web pages and detect URLs for:
- ChatGPT/OpenAI detection
- Twitter/X procrastination warnings
- Localhost broken code detection
- StackOverflow visit tracking

This permission does NOT allow us to read your browsing history or personal data.

## What We DON'T Do
- ❌ Collect personal information
- ❌ Track your browsing history
- ❌ Use analytics or tracking services
- ❌ Make external API calls
- ❌ Share data with third parties
- ❌ Sell your data
- ❌ Display ads

## Chrome Sync (Optional)
If you enable Chrome sync in your browser settings, your extension preferences may sync across your devices using Google's built-in Chrome sync infrastructure. This is:
- **Optional**: Controlled by your Chrome settings
- **Encrypted**: Uses Google's sync encryption
- **Limited**: Only syncs your custom insults and mute preferences

We do not control or have access to Chrome's sync data.

## Third-Party Services
This extension does NOT use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

## Children's Privacy
This extension does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

You can:
- Delete all stored data by uninstalling the extension
- Reset visit tracking via the extension popup
- Export your settings at any time
- Disable Chrome sync in your browser settings
